# Your voice — reading script (about 2 minutes)

Record yourself reading the lines below, then save the file in **this folder**
(`testing/khmer-voice/my-voice/`). Any format is fine: .m4a (iPhone Voice Memos),
.mp3, .wav, or .ogg/.opus (Telegram voice message).

**How to record**
- A quiet room: no fan, no music, no TV, no echo (a small room with curtains or a wardrobe is best).
- Hold the phone about 20 cm (one hand-width) from your mouth, the same distance the whole time.
- Speak in your normal selling voice, like talking to one customer: friendly and not rushed.
- Leave a short pause between lines. Mistakes are fine; just re-read the line.
